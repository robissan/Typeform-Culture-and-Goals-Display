---
name: Typeform brand references
description: Source-verification pitfall when gathering Typeform branding
---

Verify extracted logo identity against the official site header before using it.

**Why:** Brand extraction from Typeform’s homepage returned the CitizenM customer logo as the primary brand logo, while the official header contained a different Typeform symbol.

**How to apply:** Treat automated branding output as reference material, not authoritative asset identification. Check that any proposed Typeform logo belongs to Typeform rather than its customers or integrations.
